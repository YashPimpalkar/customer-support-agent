# Airline Customer Policies

## Ticket Changes
Economy tickets may be changed to a different flight up to 3 hours before
the originally scheduled departure, subject to availability in the same
fare class. A change fee may apply for Economy Basic fares; Economy
Flex, Premium Economy, Business, and First fares can be changed free of
charge. Changes within 3 hours of departure are not permitted through
self-service and must go through an agent.

## Ticket Cancellations
Tickets can be cancelled up to 3 hours before scheduled departure for a
full refund to the original form of payment on Flex, Business, and First
fares. Economy Basic fares are non-refundable but may be eligible for a
travel credit. Cancellations within 3 hours of departure are not
permitted through self-service.

## Baggage Policy
Economy passengers may check one bag up to 23kg at no charge; a second
bag incurs a fee. Business and First passengers may check two bags up to
32kg each at no charge. Carry-on baggage is limited to one personal item
and one cabin bag per passenger, subject to size limits enforced at the
gate.

## Car Rentals
Car rental reservations can be modified or cancelled free of charge up to
24 hours before the pickup time. Cancellations inside the 24-hour window
may incur a one-day rental charge. Renters must meet the minimum age and
valid license requirements of the local rental agency.

## Hotel Bookings
Hotel reservations can typically be cancelled free of charge up to 48
hours before the check-in date, unless the rate selected was explicitly
marked non-refundable at time of booking. Cancelling a non-refundable
rate forfeits the full amount paid.

## Trip Excursions and Activities
Excursions and local activity bookings can be cancelled up to 24 hours in
advance for a full refund. Weather-dependent activities (e.g. outdoor
tours) may offer a free reschedule instead of a cash refund if cancelled
by the operator.

## Special Assistance
Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance, unaccompanied minor service,
or traveling with medical equipment should request this at least 48
hours before departure so ground staff can be arranged.

## Pets
Small pets travelling in the cabin must remain in an approved carrier
that fits under the seat in front of the passenger. Larger pets can be
booked into the temperature-controlled cargo hold on select aircraft;
availability is limited and must be confirmed in advance.
